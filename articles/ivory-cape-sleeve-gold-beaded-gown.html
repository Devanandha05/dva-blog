Ivory Cape-Sleeve Wedding Gown with Gold Beaded Embroidery

A clean ivory column silhouette with long cape sleeves that fall to the floor, finished with tiny gold beaded florals. It feels regal without being heavy, and the cape means no veil is needed.

Design details

Silhouette
Fitted column with a soft flare at the hem
Neckline
Straight square neckline with beaded trim
Sleeves
Long cape-style sleeves falling from the shoulders
Fabric
Smooth ivory crepe-style fabric
Embellishment
Gold beads and pearls on the bodice, small floral sprigs on skirt and cape
Colour
Ivory with warm gold accents

Who it suits & where to wear it

The fitted shape and long cape work well for a tall or slim frame, and the cape adds movement for anyone who wants a dramatic entrance. It suits civil ceremonies, evening receptions, courthouse-to-dinner weddings and engagement shoots, anywhere you want a polished, formal look.

How to style it

  • Jewellery: Gold drop earrings or a thin gold choker to echo the beading. Skip a heavy necklace, since the neckline is already detailed.
  • Hair: A sleek low bun or soft waves pinned back keeps the shoulders and neckline visible.
  • Veil / headpiece: The cape already makes a statement. A small gold hair pin or comb is plenty.
  • Shoes & bag: Nude or champagne pointed heels and a small satin clutch in ivory or gold.
